# Homepage animation cut

Source: `animation_education_final.mov` (original recording in Downloads).

The homepage uses `doji-animation-loop.mp4` at its original playback speed:

- 12.25–18.90 seconds: two card-flip cycles, followed by the cards gathering into the center.
- 20.00–21.85 seconds: the cards spread back out and settle.

The join removes 1.10 seconds of the stationary center-logo hold. Both sides of
the join show the same resting screen; no speed adjustment or crossfade is used.
The carousel advances when the complete clip ends. This cut does not change the
education flow on the Work page.

The previous homepage cut is saved in `exports/doji-animation-previous-cut.mp4`.
